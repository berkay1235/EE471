Library App

Click the microphone and say a command, e.g. “check out item 1”.
What can I say?
  • Create: “Create item, type book, title The Catcher in the Rye, author J.D. Salinger, pages 220”
  • Create DVD: “Create item, type DVD, title Inception, director Christopher Nolan, duration 148”
  • List: “List items”, “List available books”, “List checked out DVDs”
  • Get one: “Get item 3”
  • Replace (PUT): “Replace item 3, title Dune, author Frank Herbert, pages 412”
  • Update (PATCH): “Update item 3, pages 250” or “Change the title of item 3 to Emma”
  • Delete: “Delete item 3”
  • Check out: “Check out item 3”
  • Return: “Return item 3”
